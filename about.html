Our story

Bringing a little warmth and happiness to every moment.

How it started

We've been in the hospitality business for nearly ten years as franchisees. Through all those years, we always dreamed of opening an independent café of our own, and that dream became Golden Sparrow Patisserie.

What makes us special is simple: our regular customers, genuine hospitality and the local families we've come to know and watched grow. Locals, seniors, families, tradies and the team from the centre all stop by, and every one of them is part of our story.

“It's the little things that make life beautiful.”

Our vision is to create more than just a place to enjoy good food and drinks. We want to create a welcoming space where everyone feels at home.

Whether you're stopping by for your morning coffee, catching up with friends or treating yourself to something special, we're delighted to be part of your day.
