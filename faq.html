Good questions

Everything you might want to know before you visit. Can't find your answer? Call us on 0452 209 929.

Do you offer catering for large groups?

Yes. We can organise customised platters for groups, events and the office. Get in touch to discuss your order.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes, we have a range of options to choose from. Just ask our team and we'll help you find something.

Do I need to book a table?

No booking required. Just walk in.

Where can I find Golden Sparrow Patisserie?

We're located at East Village Sunbury, just outside Woolies: 6B/2 Rhye Street, Building A, East Village, Sunbury VIC 3429.

Do you make special cakes?

Yes. We can organise cakes for all special occasions. Please get in touch ahead of time.

Do you offer takeaway?

Yes, everything on our menu is available to dine in or take away.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes, there is free parking right outside at East Village.

Are dogs welcome?

Dogs are welcome at our outdoor seating.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept card, tap and go, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash.
